Plateful: entry screen in three directions

Pair up (SCR-01), the first core screen. Same copy, same sample data, same modules in each. Toggles apply to all three.

01 Sunday paper

A large plate, a serif headline, a short column of fields. Reference: the weekend food supplement.

Strengths

  • Warmest of the three at a glance; the serif and the plate say "dinner", not "setup".
  • Single column reads top to bottom with no decisions about where to look.

Trade-offs

  • The hero plate pushes the action below the fold on a 360 phone.
  • A large image per screen sets an expectation the other screens (lists) cannot keep.
  • Cream page background, chosen deliberately here, is the one the round's rules warn against as a default.

Implementation notes

  • Hero image as a plate component with a gradient into the page; no scrim needed because text never sits on it.
  • Form fields are standard inputs with a visible label above.

02 Market ledger (recommended)

Ruled rows, tabular figures, one action docked at the thumb. Reference: the greengrocer's order book.

Strengths

  • Every core screen is a list; this model carries all six without a second layout.
  • The action is in the thumb zone on first load, at 390 and 360.
  • Large type and one accent per job: terracotta acts, sage marks what is already in.

Trade-offs

  • A single sans can read as a form; needs the headline size and the linen band to carry warmth.
  • Small plates in the margin give less of the food than the other two.

Implementation notes

  • Docked action bar is a fixed bottom element above the home indicator; the content gets bottom padding to clear it.
  • Row component reused for nights, list items and fridge items; plate as an SVG symbol with two colour variables.

03 Recipe card

A sage header, white cards in a grid, the action as the largest tile. Reference: the index-card recipe tin.

Strengths

  • The three decisions of pair-up are three visible cards; the action tile is unmissable.
  • Slab headings give it a distinct personality.

Trade-offs

  • At 360 the two-column cards get narrow; the week preview becomes a stack of same-shaped cards, the structure the round's rules warn against.
  • Sage as a surface here competes with sage as a status colour on the later screens.

Implementation notes

  • Card grid breaks to one column under 360; plates as absolutely positioned cutouts need overflow left visible.
